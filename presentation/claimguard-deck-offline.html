It's 2 AM on NH48 near Vellore. A stranded driver calls their insurer's voice agent, asks for a tow truck, and changes their mind in the same breath. Insurers are giving voice agents the power to act: open claims, dispatch trucks. Real callers are panicked, code-mixed, and correct themselves mid-sentence. Three things go wrong: the agent acts on an order the caller took back, it gives in to pressure on locked policy terms, and it repeats ID numbers into prompts and logs. Our question: how can a cheap, fast voice agent act on a live claims system safely?

Every obvious fix breaks somewhere. The keyword filter is the tempting one, but “ruko” means cancel in one sentence and “don't wait, send it fast” in another, so a filter cancels real help. Prompting harder is still probabilistic. Confirming every action slows emergencies, and panicked people say “haan haan” without listening. A bigger cloud model costs more per call, gives no guarantee, and sends raw audio and ID numbers out of the building. And there's an evaluation gap: nobody has labelled Hinglish interruption tests.

Can be interrupted

Actions are held, never fired straight away. A cancel-like word freezes them instantly; the model then decides which one was revoked. Commit at end of turn, and an ambulance never waits.

Policy engine · RAG · outbound check

Can't be bullied

The model can't write the deductible or liability. Refusals quote the policy document, and any concession or ₹ figure the policy engine didn't return is vetoed.

Local speech · masking

Won't leak

whisper.cpp runs on our own GPU, so audio never leaves the laptop. Card, Aadhaar and phone numbers are masked before the model and before telemetry.

Built on a deliberately small local model, the kind a call centre would actually run.

ClaimGuard's rule is simple: the LLM proposes, deterministic code disposes. [Point at the console.] When the caller asks for a tow, the action is held, not fired. The moment we hear “ruko”, everything held freezes. Then the model decides which action was revoked: here the tow is cancelled and the claim stays. [Click “Look-alike trap”.] Same word, “ruko mat”, and the tow still goes. Pressure can't move the deductible, because the model has no write access to it and refusals quote the policy. Speech is transcribed on our own GPU, and ID numbers are masked before anything leaves.
