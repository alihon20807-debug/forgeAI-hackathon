1. Fragility of Prompting Alone

Prompts cannot hold a reliable state machine. Under caller panic or interruption, small models suffer attention collapse and execute revoked actions.

2. Telemetry Blindspot in Production

Standard APMs monitor HTTP status and latency. They cannot cluster multi-turn failure modes, detect tool hallucination loops, or trace state drift.

3. Post-Hoc PII Exposure

Scrubbing PII after it enters model context or logs exposes raw payment cards to LLM memory and telemetry cache, violating PCI DSS v4.0.1.
